Strengthening Procurement Integrity

Evaluating vendor bids on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) requires rigorous scrutiny of technical qualifications, financial thresholds, MSME exemptions, and quality certifications. Historically, cross-referencing claims against external registries consumed an average of 14 man-hours per vendor evaluation, exposing the process to human error and potential oversight.

To address these critical vulnerabilities, the PARAKH AI platform introduces an automated mechanism that bridges isolated data silos. By extracting claims directly from unstructured tender documents and actively verifying them against central registries (MCA21, GSTN, Udyam, and Debarment databases), the platform provides procurement officers with deterministic compliance insights — reducing evaluation time by up to 85% while drastically improving audit reliability.

Strategic Objectives

  • Eradicate document forgery in public bidding
  • Automate financial threshold cross-verification
  • Ensure absolute transparency in MSME waiver claims
  • Maintain immutable cryptographic audit trails
  • Empower human officers with verified, actionable intelligence

Core Platform Architecture

A resilient ecosystem built for speed, accuracy, and absolute statutory adherence.

Tender Intelligence & Extraction

Utilizing advanced OCR and structural parsing, the platform autonomously identifies mandatory compliance clauses — such as annual turnover requirements, ISO certifications, and OEM authorizations — directly from complex PDF tender documents.

Requirement-Evidence Mapping

The BidDoc system automatically links extracted tender requirements to the specific pages and bounding boxes within a vendor's submitted evidence, establishing a traceable lineage for every claim made during the bidding process.

Multi-Source Verification (Verify+)

Through secure, real-time API integrations, the platform cross-verifies vendor submissions against authoritative government databases, instantly highlighting discrepancies between claimed financial statements and filed statutory returns.

Authorized Access Only

Access to the PARAKH AI dashboard is strictly restricted to designated Procurement Officers and authorized audit personnel within the Government e-Marketplace ecosystem.
